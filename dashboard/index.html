Workflow

This is the intended sequence for finding a trade:

1. A Telegram alert fires when price touches the EMA200 or EMA50 — this means price is at a fair-value zone, not overextended.

2. Open the dashboard. Find the pair in the grid. The grid is sorted by setup rank — the best setups appear top-left.

3. Check the pair block: are pills aligned? Is continuation above 60%? Is CMP confirming direction? Is ADX showing a trend?

4. Check SETUPS — is this pair in the top 3 ranked setups?

5. Check CALENDAR — grey border on the block means an event is coming in the next 24 hours. Read the calendar section first.

6. Long-press the pair block to open the chart. Confirm price action at the EMA level. Draw a level if the trade looks good.

Regime Card

Shows the overall market environment for three timeframes: D1 (daily), H4 (4-hour), and H1 (1-hour). Each timeframe independently votes between Risk-On, Risk-Off, Ranging, or Mixed.

Each timeframe vote is based on three signals: (1) how much stronger safe-haven currencies (USD, JPY, CHF) are vs risk-on currencies (AUD, NZD, GBP, EUR, CAD), (2) whether USD is dominant across all pairs, and (3) whether most pair pills are pointing bearishly or bullishly. If fewer than 40% of pairs show a clear direction, the regime is Ranging regardless of the other signals.

Confidence is High when all 3 signals agree, Medium when 2 agree, Low when only 1 agrees or signals conflict.

Stable means the regime hasn't changed since the last scan. Shifting means it just changed — treat with caution.

Macro Card

Tap to flip between the regime summary and a live 10-asset cross-asset grid.

W1 is the weekly macro backdrop — uses weekly percentage changes with higher thresholds so only significant moves register. D1 macro uses daily changes.

The cross-asset grid shows 10 markets and what they're doing today. Each shows value, daily change, and a direction arrow: ↑ green = rising/bullish signal for risk-on, ↓ red = falling/risk-off signal, · grey = flat.

10Y-3M spread is the yield curve — the gap between the 10-year Treasury yield and the 3-month T-bill rate. Negative means the curve is inverted, which historically precedes a recession and USD safe-haven demand. It's shown in basis points (100bp = 1%).

Bitcoin acts as a risk-appetite velocity indicator — it often moves before equities and amplifies moves in AUD, NZD, and CAD.

Catalyst

Updated every 2 hours. Reads the last 6 hours of FX headlines and asks one specific adversarial question: does any breaking news directly conflict with, accelerate, or invalidate the current top setups?

Maximum 25 words. If nothing material is found, it says "No breaking catalysts." The left accent bar turns grey when clean, red when something actionable is flagged.

Setups

The system scores every pair on 6 factors and ranks them. Pairs must have a clear daily direction and a continuation score of at least 45% to qualify. The pill badges show the top 3 pairs with direction (▲ long or ▼ short) and their score out of 10.

The text below gives a one-sentence macro/fundamental reason why each setup is ranked where it is. See the RANK SCORE section below for how the score is calculated.

Updated every 2 hours. The pair grid is sorted to match the setup ranking — the top-ranked pair appears top-left.

Calendar

High-impact FX events for the coming week, found via web search and refreshed every 6 hours. Shows forecast vs previous values so you can judge expected market impact. The note below each event is a brief interpretation: what direction does the market expect if the forecast is met?

Pair blocks get a bright grey border when their base or quote currency has a high-impact event within the next 24 hours — a warning to check here before opening a trade.

Week Ahead

Appears Sunday evening and stays until Monday evening. Claude searches the web for current weekly FX market previews and synthesises a 4-sentence strategic briefing: dominant macro theme carrying into the week, the most important scheduled event, one specific pair or currency to watch, and the biggest risk or wildcard.

Currency Strength (CSM)

The bar chart ranks 8 major currencies from weakest (left) to strongest (right) on a 0–100 scale. A score of 100 means that currency is outperforming every other currency across all pairs. A score of 0 means it's the weakest.

Tap the chart to cycle between D1 (daily), H4 (4-hour), and H1 (1-hour) views. The bar at the bottom of each pair block shows base vs quote strength on whichever timeframe is currently selected — green side winning means that side is stronger.

A large gap between base and quote (e.g. USD at 95, EUR at 20) is a strong signal that the pair has fundamental currency flow support behind the technical setup.

Pair Blocks — Pills Face (default)

Three arrows for D1, H4, H1 timeframes:

↑ bullish — price is above key EMAs, momentum is rising
↓ bearish — price is below key EMAs, momentum is falling
· neutral — no clear directional signal

A strong pill has a brighter background. ↑↑ = strongly bullish (price well above EMAs with strong MACD).

The continuation score (bottom number, 0–100%) measures how well all timeframes align. It combines five things: TF alignment (35%), entry position and pullback quality (23%), currency strength divergence between base and quote (16%), whether the macro regime supports the direction (13%), and trend strength via ADX (13%).

70%+ strong setup 50–70% moderate below 35% weak/conflicted

Pair Blocks — MOM Face (tap to flip)

The three numbers (D1 / H4 / H1) are MOM1212 scores — a proprietary momentum oscillator on a 0–100 scale for each timeframe. The small numbers below each are the delta — how much the score changed since the last scan.

60+ bullish momentum 40–60 neutral zone below 40 bearish momentum

CMP (bottom left) — Composite Momentum Position. A single 0–100 number that combines all three timeframes. This is the single most important momentum reading. Above 60 = bullish, below 40 = bearish, 40–60 = no clear momentum edge.

ADX (bottom right) — Average Directional Index. Measures trend strength regardless of direction. 25+ trending 50+ strong trend below 20 ranging/no trend. High ADX multiplies the weight of directional signals.

Setup Rank Score (0–10)

How the SETUPS ranking is calculated. Each pair gets a score from six components:

Continuation score (25%) — technical alignment quality, scaled from 45–100% → 0–10.

CMP (20%) — is momentum confirming the direction? CMP ≥ 60 for a bull setup scores 10. CMP in the 40–60 neutral zone scores 0.

D1 MOM delta (15%) — is momentum accelerating or fading? A large positive delta on a bull setup scores 10. Momentum fading against direction scores 0.

CSM divergence (20%) — how large is the strength gap between base and quote currency on D1? A gap of 50+ points scores 10. Negative gap (setup direction contradicts CSM) scores near 0.

Regime fit (10%) — does the D1 regime support the direction? Risk-Off regime supporting a USD long scores 10 × confidence multiplier.

Cross-asset (10%) — how many global markets are pushing in the same direction? e.g. WTI falling + VIX rising both support a USDCAD long simultaneously.

Hard gate: D1 pill must be directional AND continuation ≥ 45% to qualify at all.

Chart Overlay

Long-press any pair block to open the full-screen chart. Tap D1 / H4 / H1 to switch timeframes. The countdown timer shows how long until the current candle closes.

EMA 200 (white line) — long-term trend direction. Price above = bullish bias, below = bearish bias. EMA touches generate Telegram alerts.
EMA 50 (blue line) — medium-term trend. Combined with EMA200 it shows the current trend structure.
BB (grey bands) — Bollinger Bands. Wide bands = high volatility. Price at the outer band = potentially overextended.
MACD (bottom panel) — momentum. Green histogram = bullish momentum, red = bearish. Blue line crossing grey line = momentum shift.

Tap Level to draw a price level line. After drawing, tap Above or Below to set the alert direction. You'll receive a Telegram notification when price crosses that level. Tap Clear to remove all levels for this pair.

The ⧉ button copies a detailed analysis prompt to your clipboard — paste it into Claude for a fundamental macro assessment of the pair.

Data & Refresh

The dashboard auto-refreshes every 5 minutes. Technical data (pills, MOM, cont scores, CSM, regime) updates every hour. AI analysis, macro cross-assets, catalyst, setups, and calendar update every 2 hours. The calendar itself refreshes every 6 hours (events don't change that quickly). The Week Ahead is generated once on Sunday evening.

The Twelvedata API key (⚙ button) is stored only in your browser's local storage and is used solely for fetching chart OHLCV data. It never goes to GitHub Actions. The GitHub PAT is optional — only needed if you want price level alerts to sync across devices.
